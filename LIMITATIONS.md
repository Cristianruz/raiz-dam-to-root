# Limitations

Declared limitations of ROOT – From Dam to Root as of Nov 15, 2026. Sources: S9 §11, the related assumptions of S9 §4 bis, §5 and §12, S8, the decision documents in `docs/decisiones/`, and what the current build shows. Labels: **[V]** verified in a primary source · **[P]** press or secondary source · **[S]** declared assumption · **[NE]** not found.

## What the tool does not do (S9 §11)

- **It does not allocate, sell or trade water** (art. 22 of Mexico's National Water Law). It shows how much water each option asks for and for how long. The allocation and the authorized area are decided by Conagua and the irrigation modules.
- **It does not forecast the dam.** The user picks the allocation with a slider. Its three marks are press figures, not predictions: *Like 2025* [P], and *Like 2026* and *Normal year* [P], to be confirmed (`data/params.json` → `dotaciones`).
- **It does not promise that any water will be freed.** "Follow the drop" counts consumption (ET), not just withdrawals. Drip irrigation can reduce dam withdrawals and, at the same time, reduce aquifer recharge (Grafton et al. 2018, kit X3.2). The page does not advise for or against drip: it shows the drip scenario without a warning when district ET stays at or below its historical level and, if ET would rise above it, it flags the scenario ("Flagged: district consumption (ET) would exceed its historical level.").
- **It does not replace the farmer or the technician.** It supports, never replaces, their judgment. Every field sheet carries that notice.

## What "digital twin" means here

ROOT is presented as a digital twin of the pilot areas: a replica, an observed state, a model and scenarios (`docs/decisiones/2026-11-14_gemelo_digital.md`). The label has limits:

- **No field sensors.** Nothing is measured on the ground. The observed state comes from satellite and climate data.
- **No real-time data.** The twin is refreshed on each run of `python construir.py`. Between runs it does not change. `web/data/manifest.json` records the source of every figure, and its date where one is recorded; the values that still lack a reading date are listed there under `avisos` (among them the allocation marks taken from the press and the dam capacity).
- **It does not forecast the dam and does not decide for the farmer.**
- **The 3D view is a picture, not a measurement.** The relief carries a declared vertical exaggeration and the height of each water column is a visual scale (`web/data/gemelo.json` → `terreno`, `columnas.nota`).
- **The installable app works offline with the last synced copy.** Without a network it does not refresh, and the base-map tiles are not drawn.
- **Regional series do not describe a field.** SMAP, MODIS, GRACE/GRACE-FO, GLDAS, NLDAS and ERA5-Land are averages over the rectangle of an area or over the basin. Their cell sizes, as recorded in `data/processed/contexto_regional.csv` (`resolucion_m`), go from 250 m to 55,660 m. Some series rest on one or two cells per area (`n_celdas`). None of them changes the water traffic light.

## Field boundaries and crop map

- **Field boundaries are estimated.** They are Fields of The World 2025 global predictions (CC-BY-4.0), made by a neural network used outside its training domain: its benchmark does not include Mexico (kit v4, A4 and B1). They are not a cadastre.
- **Confidence filter.** Fields with FTW confidence below 50 abstain (`data/params.json` → `mapa.ftw_confianza_min`): 137 of 1,239 fields (counted in `web/data/parcelas.geojson`, `ftw_confianza`).
- **Featured fields.** The manual review against true color of the three featured fields (P0757, P0632, L0175; `web/data/parcelas.geojson` → `protagonista`) is not done yet (`docs/reparto_faltantes.md`, item D3). An AI session compared them with Sentinel-2 true color (`docs/revision_protagonistas.md`); a person still has to confirm it.
- **63 % of the fields are not classified and have no options.** 781 of 1,239 fields abstain: 583 of 940 in Delicias – Saucillo and 198 of 299 in Lázaro Cárdenas (`web/data/pruebas.json`). They are 66.2 % of the area (computed from `web/data/cubo.json` → `zona`: 16,551.54 ÷ 24,993.01 ha). The page shows them in gray, as "not classified", with no crop options.
- **Why they abstain** (`data/processed/cultivos_parcelas.csv`, `grupo_fenologico`; `web/data/parcelas.geojson`): 462 are summer annual crops that the rules cannot tell apart; 222 match no phenological group; 97 have a group but an FTW confidence below 50.
- **The 2026 crop map is rules-based.** Phenological rules on HLS decide every field (`acuerdo_metodos` = "1/1"). The 458 classified fields are fallow (219), alfalfa (118), pecan (105) and winter cereal (16) (`web/data/parcelas.geojson`). No field is classified as maize, sorghum, cotton or chile pepper: summer annual crops are left without a class.
- **No Random Forest.** A classifier needs labeled fields. The team's blind labels (`etiquetas_unidas.csv`) are not delivered, so the Random Forest and its comparison with the rules are not run (`docs/reparto_faltantes.md`, items H5 and D7).
- **No accuracy assessment.** The spatial validation (macro F1, 5 km blocks) and the Olofsson areas are not run. When they run, the reference is blind photo-interpretation, not field visits.
- **Water already committed is a minimum.** Fields without a class do not add to the perennial total, so the indicator reads "at least".

## Crop options

- **Where the options come from.** The current rotation and its alternatives come from the project's rotation table (`data/processed/opciones_b3.json`; master document §5.2 and the project's data file 27, as cited in `web/data/cubo.json` → `meta.opciones.fuente`). Of the 1,267 options on the page, 813 come from that table (`origen_opcion`: `official_table`). The other 454 are team proposals labeled "[S] team proposal, not from the agronomic guide": the table gives fewer than two alternatives for fallow and for winter cereal, so the engine completes them (forage maize and sorghum after fallow; "Oats (winter) + forage maize (summer)" after winter cereal; `cubo.json` → `meta.opciones.completadas`).
- **Young alfalfa does not get the summer irrigation cut.** The rotation table offers that cut for an established stand, never in its year of establishment. The 15 alfalfa fields whose stand is younger than 2 years show two alternatives instead of three; the 103 older stands show it (`cubo.json` → `meta.notas`; `docs/decisiones/2026-11-15_correcciones_motor.md`). The stand age comes from the field memory, a rule-based reading of greenness.
- **Pecan has no alternatives.** The 105 pecan orchards show the current rotation alone: the master document says an orchard does not rotate (`cubo.json` → `opciones_catalogo.nogal`). Kit v4 §2.3 asked for the current rotation plus two alternatives; the exception, its reason and what is missing (a minimum-irrigation scenario with a source) are in [`docs/decisiones/2026-11-15_nogal_sin_alternativas.md`](docs/decisiones/2026-11-15_nogal_sin_alternativas.md).
- **Most filters do not decide.** 809 of the 1,267 options carry filters, 1,770 filter results in all (`cubo.json` → `opciones[].filtros[].estado`). 1,667 are "needs a local check": each needs a field datum the tool does not have, or its source sets no threshold. The other 103 pass one rule decided from the field memory: the summer irrigation cut is for an established stand. No filter result is a warning in this build. A filter that was not decided is never shown as passed.
- **Frost is shown, not applied.** The page shows, next to each planting window, the probability of a frost after that date (NASA POWER, `data/processed/heladas.json`). No frost threshold discards an option.
- **Frost dates come from a grid cell, not a station.** NASA POWER is a regional cell; local frost can be more frequent. The comparison with a weather station was not run (`data/processed/heladas.json` → `nota`, `estacion_smn`).
- **Drip scenario: indicative efficiencies, not local.** "Follow the drop" shows a drip scenario with the field application efficiencies of FAO's Irrigation Water Management Training Manual No. 4 (Brouwer, Prins and Heibloem 1989), Annex I, Table 8: 0.9 for drip and 0.6 for surface irrigation, labeled "[V] indicative, not local" (`data/processed/supuestos.json` → `ea_goteo`, `ea_superficial_referencia`; `cubo.json` → `meta.goteo`). They were not measured in DR-005. `data/params.json` → `eficiencia.goteo` stays null: the value lives in `supuestos.json`. Without a consumption cap, the aquifer return is an upper bound (`meta.goteo.retorno_sin_tope_es_cota_superior`).
- **Farmer priorities are highlighted, not combined.** The challenge asks to combine the farmer's priorities. ROOT sets the options side by side, each with Water, Soil and Money rows, and "What matters most to you?" highlights options by the priority you pick; it does not rank them. It applies no weights, gives no score and marks no option as the best: the farmer and the technician weigh. The priorities it can back are the ones the data hold (water need, water term, nitrogen fixing, bare-soil days); forage value, organic matter and market are listed as "no data in ROOT yet" (`web/js/prioridades.js`).
- **Money row: empty.** There is no verified margin data (`web/data/cubo.json` → `meta.notas`; `dinero` is null in every option). The row shows "No verified data". The state crop reports we read do not include costs or prices (`docs/referencias/ref_cultivos_sdr.md`).

## Water demand

- **Field (on-farm) efficiency is assumed, not measured.** The demand model uses a declared range of 0.45–0.70 [S], with no measured value for DR-005 (`data/processed/rotaciones.json` → `params.ea_gravedad`). `data/params.json` → `eficiencia.parcelaria` stays null until a sourced value arrives. The P10–P90 band carries this uncertainty.
- **Model depth against INIFAP.** For alfalfa the model's gross field depth is 28.4 millares/ha at P50 (P10–P90: 23.13–35.16) in Delicias – Saucillo and 27.83 in Lázaro Cárdenas (`data/processed/rotaciones.json`, `rotaciones_lazaro_cardenas.json`; rain 0 %). INIFAP's depth is 17.5 millares/ha [V] (S9 §5). The model's net depth (before dividing by the efficiency) is 1,626 mm, that is 16.26 millares/ha. The gap does not come from the efficiency alone: the model's alfalfa ETc is 1,748 mm, against 1,139 mm estimated for DR-005 (Villalobos-Cano et al. 2024, 2009–2016 means, Hargreaves), and with an efficiency of 0.60–0.70 the depth would still be 25.1 millares/ha, above the 17.4 applied in the district (`docs/modelo_vs_practica.md` §2, point 3, and §3).
- **Net depth against INIFAP (own calculation).** In alfalfa, cotton and forage maize, the model's net depth at P50 is close to INIFAP's depth: 16.26 against 17.5 millares/ha, 7.20 against 6.8 and 5.40 against 5.5 (`rotaciones.json`, Delicias – Saucillo, rain 0 %; INIFAP [V] from S9 §5). INIFAP's depth already includes field losses, so this is a contrast, not a calibration: it does not split the gap between crop ET and efficiency.
- **Local reference depth.** For alfalfa and pecan the field sheet also shows the depth applied in the district, 2009–2016 means [V]: 17.42 and 13.72 millares/ha (Villalobos-Cano et al. 2024; `cubo.json` → `agua.lamina_local`); for forage maize, INIFAP's recommendation of 5.0–6.0. They are district averages or regional recommendations, not measurements of the field, and the study does not say where the applied water was measured. The traffic light keeps using the model's need.
- **Other tests point the same way.** In the H1 back-test the model's demand for the cycle is close to twice the authorized volume (1,185.7 hm³ against 600 hm³). In the ET contrast the modeled ETc is 53 % above SSEBop on the original rectangle (`docs/resultado_H1.md`, `docs/resultado_A8.md`). ECOSTRESS reads lower than the modeled ETc on clear days; part of that gap can come from how the product builds its daily value, so it is not counted as evidence of how much water the district uses (`docs/ecostress.md`, `docs/revisiones/2026-11-15_ecostress.md`). The model computes the full demand of a well-watered crop; the district operates with less. If the efficiency range is too low, hectares covered are understated and committed water is overstated. We will ask a district technician.
- **A possible ETo bias is an assumption.** The model tests a bias of −10 % to 0 % in the reference ET [S], with no source in the repo (`rotaciones.json` → `params.sesgo_eto`).
- **Pecan.** The FAO-56 "Walnut orchard" Kc [V] is adjusted with an assumed canopy cover of 0.7 [S] (`rotaciones.json` → `params.cobertura_dosel_nogal`), not measured with NDVI yet (S8, decision 5). The pecan term is a 10-year calculation horizon [S], not the life of the orchard.
- **Alfalfa age.** The stand age is counted from the year of establishment, detected with a minimum monthly NDVI below 0.25 [S] (`data/processed/fuentes.json`, entry `cultivos_parcelas.csv`). The stand life of 3–4 years is labeled [P] (FIRA AGROCOSTOS 2025).
- **Where the "millar" is measured:** assumed at the field intake [S]; to be confirmed with a module technician. *Like 2026* and *Normal year* are [P], to be confirmed, because their article was not read directly (`data/params.json` → `dotaciones`).
- **NASA POWER is one grid cell per area**, not a station (MERRA-2, 0.5° × 0.625°). The two areas fall in different cells, so each has its own climate and demand files (`data/processed/fuentes.json`).
- **OpenET does not cover Mexico.** The regional ET contrast uses SSEBop on VIIRS.

## Relief and storm

- **NASADEM at 30 m is used at area scale, not per field.** Gravity-irrigated fields are leveled, and 30 m does not resolve their inner detail. The layer is labeled "regional, 30 m (NASADEM); not field drainage design" (`web/data/relieve.json`).
- **"Simulate a storm" is illustrative.** No hydrograph, no depths, no earthworks and no drain design.
- **Per-field relief indicators sit within the noise of the elevation model in small fields.** The Soil row shows a fitted slope, the largest single inflow from outside the field (a minimum: nothing outside the mapped area is counted) and the share of the field in closed pits of the elevation model. On the flat valley floor, most route directions come from filling small pits and flats of the model, so the lines are approximate (`web/js/relieve.js`).
- **Runoff routes are not drawn inside closed depressions of the elevation model.** Where the model shows a closed depression 3 m deep or more [S], the 30 m data cannot tell where water leaves, so no route is drawn there. This omits 2,931 route cells and 339 short stretches in Delicias – Saucillo, and 384 cells and 49 stretches in Lázaro Cárdenas (`data/processed/relieve_meta.json` and `relieve_meta_lazaro_cardenas.json` → `rutas_depresiones`). A route that stops on the map is a limit of the data, not a statement that the water stops.
- **A route is drawn from 50 ha of contributing area** [S], a display threshold (`web/data/relieve.json` → `umbral_ruta_ha`).
- **The storm depth is one number per area.** It is the 95th percentile of the days with rain of 1 mm or more in GPM IMERG V07, averaged over the area rectangle, 2001-01-01 to 2026-11-13: 20.2 mm in Delicias – Saucillo and 20.7 mm in Lázaro Cárdenas (`web/data/relieve.json`). The same percentile from NASA POWER, 1984-01-01 to 2026-11-09, gives 15.5 mm and 15.9 mm (`relieve_meta*.json` → `tormenta.contraste`). The two sources differ.
- **No LiDAR.** INEGI LiDAR coverage of the pilot areas was not confirmed [NE], so there is no ponding analysis inside fields.

## Field memory and tests

- **Field memory** is a rule-based reading of monthly greenness (NDVI threshold 0.40, sensitivity 0.35–0.45 [S]; `data/params.json` → `memoria`). It is not a crop census.
- **H1 does not meet the criterion declared before the analysis** (S9 §6; operating points in `docs/h1_especificacion_operativa.md`, commit `b9baced`, before the model was run). There is no public OSF registration in this repository yet; the page says "criterion declared before the analysis" (`web/data/pruebas.json`). At Jul 14, 2026 the model's P50 is 904.6 hm³ against a truth of 447.65 hm³; baselines L1-s (404.5) and L1 (367.2) were closer (`docs/resultado_H1.md`). Limits of that test:
  - It is a partial back-test: the official closing figures of the 2026 cycle are not published.
  - The truth is press-reported and equals the storage drop, which includes evaporation and inflows. The SIH series has 18 zero days, so its integral (296.31 hm³) is a floor (`docs/h1_verdad_sih.md`).
  - The press figures and the SIH series were already known when the operating points were fixed (`docs/h1_especificacion_operativa.md`).
  - The session that ran the analysis did not have the team's preregistration text in view (document 30, not in this repository). If that text fixes something different, it prevails and the analysis is repeated.
  - One cycle does not support claims of accuracy or significance.
- **H3 is not supported.** Among 184 annual-crop fields the share with an active summer crop was 82.6 % in 2025 against a median of 84.2 % (−1.6 points; the criterion asked for −25) (`docs/resultado_H3.md`). Limits of that test:
  - It compares shares, not fields. Satellite greenness does not tell the source of the water; wells, weeds or monsoon rain can also reach the NDVI threshold.
  - Survivorship: orchards are identified in 2026, so orchards removed before 2026 are not observed.
  - The registered analysis is on the original rectangle (338 fields). The results on the two pilot areas are post hoc, because the areas changed after that result was known.
- **Figure checker tested on templates, not on an LLM.** 30 of 30 golden-set cases correct and 0 of 71 numbers altered (`docs/figuras/llm_eval.csv`). There is no LLM API key, so every explanation is a template with placeholders. The checker has not run on LLM-written text.
- **ET contrast: regional, one source.** The SSEBop comparison is not a field-level validation. Modeled ETc is a potential demand; SSEBop is actual ET averaged over the whole rectangle, including fields without a class and non-farm land. The registered contrast covers the original rectangle; the two areas are post hoc (`docs/resultado_A8.md`). WaPOR v3 was not run.
- **The FAO-56 test covers just the reference ET.** `tests/test_fao56_manual.py` passes in every build (`web/data/pruebas.json`): it checks Example 8 (extraterrestrial radiation) and Example 18 (daily ETo) of the manual. It does not test the crop coefficients (Kc), the effective rain or the net depth.
- **Aquifer figure:** the availability of −167.12 hm³/year is Conagua's official deficit [V], not a measured loss.

## Regional context, canopy height and soil

- **GRACE/GRACE-FO is one basin series.** It measures all stored water together (surface, soil and aquifer) and does not separate the aquifer or the district. The border between two 3° mascons crosses the pilot areas, so a difference between areas would be a grid effect (`docs/decisiones/2026-11-14_recursos_del_reto.md`). The delivered series ends in 2024-10 (`data/processed/contexto_regional.csv`).
- **GEDI samples with separate shots.** 167 of 1,239 fields have no shot (computed from `data/processed/fuentes.json`: 1,072 fields with at least one). That is "no data", not zero.
- **ERA5-Land is not a NASA product** (ECMWF and Copernicus).
- **Soil comes from global models, not from samples.** SoilGrids 2.0 (250 m) is a mapped prediction; no soil was sampled in these fields. Its texture class and OpenLandMap's agree in 584 of 1,238 fields with data in both (`web/data/suelo.json` → `coincidencia_textura`). The suitability of the Soil row compares the map with published crop ranges (FAO ECOCROP), not with a local trial, and never discards or reorders an option.
- **The soil type is a regional reading.** The field sheet shows a USDA great group for each field from OpenLandMap (250 m; Hengl and Nauman 2018), labeled "Regional soil type from a global map, not a soil test". It is the prediction of a global model, not a soil survey, and it can differ from the soils that the municipal plan lists, because they come from different maps (`web/data/suelo.json` → `tipo_suelo`).
- **Nutrient notes are a risk by rule, not a diagnosis.** "Nutrient notes" apply published rules (NOM-021-SEMARNAT-2000, Montana State University Extension, NMSU, Illinois) to the pH, organic carbon, CEC and texture that SoilGrids maps for the field, labeled "Rule-based risk from a global soil map; needs a soil test". No nutrient was measured in these fields: phosphorus, potassium, iron and zinc need a soil test. Mapped nitrogen is not used, because the map gives a carbon-to-nitrogen ratio that is not credible (`data/processed/fuentes.json`, entries `suelo_parcelas.csv` and `suelo_nutrientes_parcelas.csv`; `docs/suelo_tipos_y_nutrientes.md`).
- **ECOSTRESS sees few clear days.** It is ET at 70 m on clear-sky passes (`data/processed/ecostress_parcelas.csv`, column `n_escenas`, the number of clear overpasses). Some months have no clear overpass, and a field's value can rest on one or two overpasses. It is satellite ET on those days, not a field measurement, and it is not attributed to a cause (`docs/ecostress.md`). Each value is the median of the clear overpasses of the month, after leaving out the passes near sunrise or sunset and the scenes with the area under cloud; those thresholds are assumptions [S] (`data/processed/supuestos.json`). The overpasses of one month can still differ from each other, and the geolocation of each scene was not checked (`docs/ecostress.md`; independent review: `docs/revisiones/2026-11-15_ecostress.md`).
- **GAEZ describes the area, not the field.** Its cells are about 9 km (`data/processed/gaez_zona.csv`); FAO recommends treating the results conservatively, at aggregation levels that match the resolution of the base data (`datos/colab/G1_gaez_zona.py`). GAEZ v4 does not assess chile or pecan, and its maize layer is grain maize, used as a stand-in for forage maize.
- **None of these series forecasts the dam.**

## Pilot areas and data status

- **Pilot rectangles are design choices [S].** The module boundary is not public [NE]. The two areas are `delicias_saucillo` (940 fields, 19,145.19 ha) and `lazaro_cardenas` (299 fields, 5,847.82 ha) (`web/data/cubo.json` → `zonas`; `docs/decisiones/2026-11-14_dos_zonas.md`). They may not represent the whole district.
- **The team's reference localities are points, not fields.**
- **The web reads real deliveries** (`web/data/cubo.json` → `meta.mock: false`). Still missing from the team: the allocation table (`dotaciones.json`) and the figure table (`cifras.csv`) (`docs/reparto_faltantes.md`, items D4 and I7).
- **Public site.** https://cristianruz.github.io/raiz-dam-to-root/ publishes `main` (GitHub Pages); pull request #4, with the version described here, is merged.
- With few fields in a group, its shares rest on few fields.
